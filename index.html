WHO WE ARE

Nurturing Ethical, Visionary Leaders

The Guild Presidents Leadership Academy (GPLA) is a non-partisan leadership development and regional convening platform committed to nurturing ethical, visionary and transformational leaders across East Africa.

Through leadership training, mentorship, policy dialogue, regional summits, research, institutional partnerships and community-based initiatives, GPLA brings together young leaders, governments, universities, development partners, the private sector and experienced statespersons to address critical governance and development challenges.

While university and student leadership remain central to our identity, GPLA's work has expanded to include public leadership, regional integration, innovation, entrepreneurship, civic participation, institutional development and sustainable socio-economic transformation.

GPLA is headquartered in Uganda and works across East Africa to build bridges between generations, institutions and countries, transforming youthful leadership potential into responsible service, practical solutions and lasting regional impact.

FOUR FLAGSHIP PROGRAMMES

Foundational Programme

Guild Leadership Development & Mentorship Programme

Preparing Student Leaders for Responsible Service and Future Leadership. The programme provides newly elected and serving student leaders with practical training in ethical leadership, financial accountability, negotiation, communication, conflict resolution, policy engagement and responsible representation. It also supports leadership transition, institutional continuity and mentorship by connecting current guild leaders with former guild presidents, public officials, professionals and experienced national leaders.

Strategic Objectives

  • Equip guild leaders with practical leadership and governance skills
  • Promote integrity, accountability and responsible student representation
  • Strengthen cooperation between student leaders and university administrations
  • Improve leadership transition and institutional continuity within guild governments
  • Connect current guild leaders with mentors and leadership alumni
  • Prepare student leaders for future roles in public service and national development

Flagship Programme · Innovation

Uganda National Science Park & Innovation Area Initiative

Harnessing Youth Innovation for Production and National Transformation. The Initiative seeks to establish a nationally supported ecosystem where universities, researchers, innovators, entrepreneurs, industries, investors and government institutions can collaborate to transform ideas and research into commercially viable products, enterprises and employment opportunities.

Priority Areas

  • Agricultural technology and agro-processing
  • Artificial intelligence and digital innovation
  • Health and biotechnology
  • Renewable energy and climate technology
  • Engineering and advanced manufacturing
  • Minerals, oil and gas technologies
  • Creative and knowledge-based industries
  • Research commercialisation and technology transfer

Flagship Programme · Leadership

GPLA Executive Master Classes

Developing Ethical, Strategic and Transformational Leaders. The programme brings together distinguished statespersons, experienced public servants, diplomats, academics, business leaders and governance practitioners to share practical knowledge, leadership experience and institutional lessons. Focused on managing public trust, making difficult decisions, leading during crises, strengthening institutions and delivering results in complex environments.

Core Learning Areas

  • Ethical leadership and values-based decision-making
  • Leadership in public service
  • Governance, public administration and statecraft
  • Managing public trust and institutional reputation
  • Leadership during crisis and change
  • Diplomacy, geopolitics and regional integration
  • Strategic communication and stakeholder management
  • Institutional performance and succession planning

Flagship Programme · Annual

East African Guild Leaders Summit

Advancing Youth Leadership and East African Integration. GPLA's flagship regional convening platform, bringing together university guild leaders, policymakers, academic leaders, development partners, private-sector actors and distinguished statespersons from across East Africa.

Strategic Objectives

  • Promote youth participation in the East African integration agenda
  • Strengthen cooperation among university student leaders across the region
  • Build young leaders' understanding of regional institutions, diplomacy and governance
  • Facilitate dialogue between young people, governments, universities and the private sector
  • Develop a sustainable network of emerging East African leaders
  • Generate youth-led recommendations on regional policy and development
