WHO WE ARE

Nurturing Ethical, Visionary Leaders

The Guild Presidents Leadership Academy (GPLA) is a non-partisan leadership development and regional convening platform committed to nurturing ethical, visionary and transformational leaders across East Africa.

Through leadership training, mentorship, policy dialogue, regional summits, research, institutional partnerships and community-based initiatives, GPLA brings together young leaders, governments, universities, development partners, the private sector and experienced statespersons to address critical governance and development challenges.

GPLA also provides an integrated platform for dialogue on governance, education, technology, regional trade, youth employment, peace, mobility and sustainable development. It connects current student leaders with experienced national and regional leaders, strengthening intergenerational learning and building a network of young people committed to a united, peaceful and prosperous East Africa.

Our Programs

SIX PATHWAYS TO TRANSFORM EAST AFRICA

5 Days · Residential

Transformational Leadership Bootcamp (TLB)

The programme provides newly elected and serving student leaders with practical training in ethical leadership, financial accountability, negotiation, communication, conflict resolution, policy engagement and responsible representation. It also supports leadership transition, institutional continuity and mentorship by connecting current guild leaders with former guild presidents, public officials, professionals and experienced national leaders.

Strategic Objectives

  • Equip guild leaders with practical leadership and governance skills
  • Promote integrity, accountability and responsible student representation
  • Strengthen cooperation between student leaders and university administrations
  • Improve leadership transition and institutional continuity within guild governments
  • Connect current guild leaders with mentors and leadership alumni

Innovation & Production Lab (IPL)

A platform for incubation, research commercialisation, industrial production, skills development, technology transfer and investment in sectors critical to Uganda's development.
