Event Description (for Luma "About" section)

The 3rd Annual East African Guild Leaders' Summit convened by the Guild Presidents Leadership Academy (GPLA) is the premier regional platform for student leadership, democratic governance, and integration dialogue in East Africa. Bringing together over 700 Guild Presidents, student leaders, policymakers, and heads of state from across the East African Community, this two-day Summit will be held at the historic Freedom Square, Makerere University — the birthplace of African student activism.

☛ THEME:

"Youth Leadership, Production, and Regional Integration: Building a Competitive East Africa in a Changing Global Order"

☛ CHIEF GUEST:

H.E. Yoweri Kaguta Museveni, President of the Republic of Uganda and Chairperson of the East African Community, will deliver the keynote address on the future of East African integration and youth leadership.

☛ SUMMIT LEGACY & PATRONAGE:

The Guild Leaders' Summit has grown into a defining Pan-African youth leadership movement. Previous editions were graced by:

  • 2025: H.E. Uhuru Kenyatta, Former President of the Republic of Kenya
  • 2024: H.E. Jakaya Mrisho Kikwete, Former President of the United Republic of Tanzania (also serves as Summit Patron)

The Academy is guided by Dr. Ruhakana Rugunda, former Prime Minister of Uganda, as Patron of GPLA, and Hon. Canon Matthew Rukikaire as a key member of its Advisory Board. The growing GPLA network spans Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, and Rwanda.

☛ WHY THIS MATTERS:

East Africa stands at a critical juncture — shaped by shifting global geopolitics, the urgent need for regional self-reliance, and the imperative of economic transformation. With over 70% of the region's population under 30, the success of integration and production agendas depends on how effectively young leaders are mobilized, organized, and oriented toward innovation, enterprise, and regional thinking.

University Guild Presidents occupy a unique position as structured leaders with constituencies, legitimacy, and influence within institutions that shape future policymakers, entrepreneurs, and public servants. This Summit aligns youth leadership with the region's strategic priorities — integration, production, markets, and competitiveness.

☛ SUMMIT STRUCTURE:

Day One — Markets, Innovation and Youth Opportunity

  • Panel 1: Enhancing Trade through East African Integration — Challenges and Opportunities
  • Panel 2: AFCON 2027 as a Platform for Youth Economic Participation
  • Panel 3: University Leaders Dialogue — Education and Regional Mobility
  • Panel 4: Agri-tech Innovations for Youth-led Solutions in Regional Food Security and Climate Justice
  • Adoption of the Makerere Youth Declaration on Integration and Production

Day Two — Integration, Leadership and Strategic Direction

  • Opening Ceremony featuring heads of state and regional dignitaries
  • Chief Guest Address: The Future of East African Integration and Youth Leadership
  • Panel: Youth, Production and National Development
  • Intergenerational Engagement with Policy Leaders

☛ FOCUS AREAS:

  1. Political and Economic Integration — Understanding the strategic importance of a united East Africa in strengthening markets, security, and global competitiveness.
  2. Youth, Production and Industrial Transformation — Shifting youth focus from job-seeking to production, value addition, and enterprise development.
  3. Regional Markets, Trade and Economic Opportunities — Positioning young people to participate in cross-border trade, regional value chains, and intra-African commerce.
  4. Sports Diplomacy and Regional Integration (AFCON 2027) — Leveraging the joint EAC hosting of AFCON 2027 for youth employment, infrastructure development, and regional branding.
  5. Technology, Innovation and the Digital Economy — Harnessing digital tools to enhance productivity, governance, and economic participation.
  6. Education, Mobility and Knowledge Integration — Strengthening collaboration among universities to support skills development, research, and regional knowledge systems.

☛ EXPECTED OUTCOMES:

  • Makerere Youth Declaration on East African Integration and Production
  • Youth Policy Proposals on Trade, Markets, and Economic Participation
  • Regional Student Leadership Network Strengthening
  • Action Plans for Campus-based Implementation
  • Summit Report and Policy Briefs

☛ WHO SHOULD ATTEND:

  • Current and former Guild Presidents from universities across East Africa
  • Student leaders and student government officials
  • Youth policymakers, entrepreneurs, and innovators
  • Academics, researchers, and university administrators
  • Civil society and development partners working on youth and governance

📋 Registration Form Fields

On Luma, go to Event Settings → Registration → Custom Questions and add these exactly as shown:

  • 1. Full Name Type: Short Text · Required · Label: "Full Name"
  • 2. Country Type: Dropdown/Select · Required · Options: Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, Burundi, South Sudan, Other
  • 3. National ID/Passport Number Type: Short Text · Required · Label: "National ID / Passport Number"
  • 4. Institution Type: Short Text · Required · Label: "Institution (University/Organization)"
  • 5. Leadership Position/Role Type: Short Text · Required · Label: "Leadership Position/Role" · Placeholder: e.g. Guild President, Prime Minister, Speaker
💡 Pro Tip: On Luma, you can also add optional questions after these to capture dietary restrictions, T-shirt size, or accommodation needs for planning purposes.

🎟️ Tickets & Pricing

Recommended Setup (Free)

1. General Admission (Free) — For all delegates. Set to $0. Requires approval? No (or Yes if you want to vet).
2. VIP / Special Guest — Optional. Invite-only, hidden from public.
3. Capacity: Set a realistic cap. If expecting 700+, set capacity to 800 to allow buffer.
💰 Note: Since this is a free event, Luma charges no platform fee. If you later decide to charge a nominal fee (for certificates/merchandise), use Luma Plus to get 0% platform fee.

🚀 Step-by-Step Luma Setup

1. Go to luma.com/create and sign in (or create a GPLA account)
2. Select "Create Event" → "In-Person Event"
3. Event Name: "3rd Annual East African Guild Leaders' Summit 2026"
4. Date & Time: 23 July 2026, 8:00 AM → 24 July 2026, 5:00 PM (EAT, UTC+3)
5. Location: "Freedom Square, Makerere University, Kampala, Uganda"
6. Upload Cover Image — Use the flyer/screenshot from above
7. About Section: Paste the event description from this guide
8. Tickets: Set up Free General Admission ticket type
