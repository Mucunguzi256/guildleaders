About Us

Shaping East Africa's Future Leaders

The Guild Presidents Leadership Academy (GPLA) is a non-partisan leadership development and regional convening platform committed to nurturing ethical, visionary and transformational leaders across East Africa.

Established from the recognition that university guild leaders are an important foundation for future public, private and civic leadership, GPLA equips emerging leaders with the knowledge, values, networks and practical skills required to serve society responsibly and effectively.

Through leadership training, mentorship, policy dialogue, regional summits, research, institutional partnerships and community-based initiatives, GPLA brings together young leaders, governments, universities, development partners, the private sector and experienced statespersons to address critical governance and development challenges.

While university and student leadership remain central to our identity, GPLA's work has expanded to include public leadership, regional integration, innovation, entrepreneurship, civic participation, institutional development and sustainable socio-economic transformation.

GPLA is headquartered in Uganda and works across East Africa to build bridges between generations, institutions and countries, transforming youthful leadership potential into responsible service, practical solutions and lasting regional impact.
